How We Organize

The 5-Member Rotating Leadership Model

AMOY operates without rigid hierarchy. Instead of a permanent boss, project leadership rotates dynamically depending on the primary problem our SME client faces.

1

Project & Operations Lead

Core Focus: Process performance, Lean Six Sigma methodologies, workflow coordination, operational waste reduction.

Trigger: Leads when the client faces operational delays, slow order fulfillment, or capacity bottlenecks.
2

Data & Analytics Lead

Core Focus: Turning fragmented data into measurable evidence via Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI visualization.

Trigger: Leads when the client lacks performance visibility or needs predictive and quantitative auditing.
3

Client Systems Lead

Core Focus: Managing client acquisition pipelines, customer journey architecture, and CRM system setup.

Trigger: Leads when incoming leads are slipping through cracks or customer data is unorganized.
4

Marketing & Growth Lead

Core Focus: Market positioning, customer segmentation, digital presence, and measuring channel ROI.

Trigger: Leads when an enterprise requires market expansion or customer profiling alignment.
5

Finance & Economic Impact Lead

Core Focus: Evaluating cost structures, break-even analysis, ROI modeling, and bottom-line economic validation.

Trigger: Leads when client faces margin erosion or needs financial proof before adopting technology.

Unified Team Execution

While one lead takes accountability for client interfacing and final delivery, all five domain leads contribute their specialized expertise to every project.
